TITLE:   deadline
STYLE:   Black & white manga

=== characters ===
Fumika: an adult manga artist in her twenties with glossy black hair in a messy low ponytail with one light-blue streak in her bangs, light-blue eyes, white oversized shirt with rolled-up sleeves, black ribbon tie, black pleated skirt
Sora: her editor, an adult woman with short silver hair and glasses, navy blazer, white blouse, grey pencil skirt
Casual Fumika: Fumika, an adult woman in her twenties with glossy black hair in a messy low ponytail with one light-blue streak in her bangs, light-blue eyes, in a grey oversized hoodie and black shorts

=== story ===
Fumika grins at the viewer holding up a dip pen in front of a pile of manga pages | #DEADLINE! | ##by Fumika

Fumika sags over a blank manga page at her desk late at night, a cold coffee mug beside her | Nothing...
Her phone lights up on the desk with a message from Sora | !BZZT
Close-up of Fumika, panicking | Seven pages by tomorrow?!

Fumika stares out of the window at the city lights, deep in thought | ...
Fumika's eyes light up, a light bulb above her head | That's it!
Fumika inks a page at full speed, ink drops flying | !SCRATCH SCRATCH

Morning sun through the window, Fumika asleep on her desk on a stack of finished pages | Zzz...
Sora opens the door holding two coffees, surprised | Fumika...?
Sora smiles at the finished pages, Fumika gives a sleepy thumbs up | Made it...

Casual Fumika walks out into the morning street, stretching, the sun rising behind the buildings | Free at last!
Close-up of Casual Fumika yawning | ...Bed.
